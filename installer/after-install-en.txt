AmpCast is installed. Four more steps:

1. VIRTUAL CABLE (once)
   Discord hears AmpCast through a virtual audio device.
   If you don't have one yet, install the free VB-CABLE:
   https://vb-audio.com/Cable/  (run VBCABLE_Setup_x64.exe as administrator,
   then restart the PC).

2. AMPCAST
   Pick your audio interface, your microphone and the "CABLE Input" output.

3. DAW (FL Studio, Ableton, Studio One, Reaper, Cubase...)
   Audio driver: "AmpCast ASIO", 48 kHz, buffer 64-128.
   If the DAW was open during setup, restart it.

4. DISCORD > Settings > Voice & Video
   Input device: "CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)".
   Turn off noise suppression, echo cancellation and automatic gain control.
   Then press "Broadcast to Discord" in AmpCast.

STEREO. Discord voice channels are always mono. For stereo DAW sound:
   turn on "Stereo via screen share" in AmpCast,
   set the Discord input device to your microphone (not the cable),
   Share Screen > Applications > AmpCast, with sound on.
   Keep the AmpCast window open (minimizing is fine).

In normal mode you can close the AmpCast window; it keeps running in the tray.
